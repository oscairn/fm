# Coverage

What the engine plays of the model, parameter by parameter: "played", or the milestone that
brings it (docs/design.md, section 7). `crates/fm-core/src/coverage.rs` holds the same status and a
test checks that every parameter has exactly one row here with that status. For a sound,
`fmbench coverage SOUND` lists its parameters at non-default values that are not played
("not covered: ID=VALUE: why"); the default of every parameter is played.

Operator parameters are listed once, as `opN.name` (operators 1-8, ids 1000 + 100 op + k with op
from 0). Range is the raw range and (the default). "Existing profile" figures are the existing
FM plugin profile's measured accuracy against the reference (docs/design.md, section 1), which
the laws here reuse; "the engine" figures are this repository's tests. Known differences from the
reference: docs/known-differences.md.

At M5: the laws below are played on every routing, the operator forms, pitch, the voice LFO, the
sound LFO, the macros, level scaling, the part's envelope offsets, the filter with its envelope
and pan included; nothing else. A sound LFO destination with depth that is not played (an
effect's parameter) and a stored filter type that is none of the model's are reported by
`fmbench coverage` beside the parameters. The filter's coefficients and pan are evaluated every
16 frames and ramped a frame (docs/design.md, 3.7). Pitch that moves (both pitch envelopes,
portamento, bend, the voice and sound LFOs) is
evaluated every 16 frames, the increments ramped linearly across a step while something moves them
(docs/design.md, 2.3); the pitch a note keeps is on the grid of 1/2048 octave, what moves is not.

At M6 the plugin (`docs/plugin.md`) offers every parameter below as a CLAP parameter with its id and
plays each as the core does; what the plugin itself does not cover (the tuning expression, CC 65,
parameter modulation) is listed there.

At M7: the channel controllers (mod wheel, breath, both foot controllers, aftertouch, expression,
the sostenuto pedal) and each note's volume expression, and the controller boxes (`boxN_*`) on the
operator level and frequency (each operator's `ctrl_setN`; the frequency by its
`pitch_ctrl_sens`), the cutoff and the resonance, the voice LFO's speed, pitch depth and amplitude
depth, and the sound's pitch (and since 0.6.3 the voice LFO's filter depth, the part's amplitude
envelope offsets, the feedback, the operators' form, skirt, resonance, envelope times and the level by
the level controller sensitivity; since 0.6.4 the operators' detune and pitch envelope decay, mono/poly,
pan, the filter envelope's depth, the portamento time, the sound LFO's speed and depths; since 0.6.6
the voice LFO's speed in its extended range, the filter envelope's times, the common pitch envelope's
attack and release times and the operators' pitch envelope attack), evaluated when a source moves and ramped across the next control
step (docs/design.md, 3.9). A box on any other destination, from a source the host does not send,
or on a curve not measured (played as linear) is reported by `fmbench coverage` beside the
parameters, by its `boxN_dst`. Not played yet: the level controller sensitivity, the key
controller sets, micro tuning, the sound LFO's random speed (measured, its law not resolved).
